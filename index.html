GITAM x Bower Innovation Challenge 2026

Dear [First Name],

Why do good student ideas stall? Rarely for lack of talent. Almost always for one of four reasons.

  1. No mentor. Nobody experienced is questioning the assumptions.
  2. No pitch practice. The idea is clear in your head and muddled on the slide.
  3. No industry or investor access. Nobody who has seen ventures grow is listening.
  4. No structure. Without a deadline, "next month" becomes never.

GIC is built to remove each barrier.

  • Mentor: every advancing team gets a personal mentor during the online bootcamp, 9-11 November.
  • Practice: a Round 2 deck, a 20-minute online semi-final pitch and, for finalists, a live stage in Hyderabad.
  • Access: the Winner gets access to the GITAM VDC investor and alumni ecosystem, every Senior finalist receives a year of 1:1 venture coaching, and the Finale includes an industry keynote and networking.
  • Structure: a fixed calendar and a Pitch Deck Template you can download from the website.

An idea needs a deadline to become a venture. This one is 31 October, 11:59 PM IST.
